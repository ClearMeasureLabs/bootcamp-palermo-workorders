# Video branding assets

Brand images used by every AI Factory demo video rendered for this repository.

| File | What it is | Where it goes in a video |
|------|------------|--------------------------|
| `ai-factory-bot.png` | AI Factory bot mark (474×248, transparent) | **Top-left corner** of every content frame |
| `clear-measure-logo.png` | Clear Measure logo, "Empowering Software Delivery" (1966×618, white background) | **Top-right corner** of every content frame |

## Placement rules

**Corner watermarks (every content frame).** Both images are shown small, one in each
top corner, sized so they never obstruct the content being demonstrated. A height of
roughly 5–7% of the frame (about 60–80 px tall at 1080p) with a comfortable margin from the
edges is right. They are decoration: the screenshot underneath is the point of the frame.

**Closing card (the final frames of the video).** The video ends on a white background with
larger versions of both images side by side, bot on the left and logo on the right, roughly
centred and vertically aligned, held for about three seconds. This is the only frame where the
images are large and the only frame with no product content.

Both PNGs are small enough to load directly from this folder; do not re-encode or recolour
them.
